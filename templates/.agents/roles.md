## Role guide
- maintainer — final say on architecture and ADRs; merges and releases.
- dev — builds features; asks for review on every change.
- reviewer — reviews before merge; sets `review` in plan.csv.
- qa — checks that done really meets the DoD.
- pm — keeps plan.csv and the external tracker current.
- researcher — runs experiments; owns result ADRs.
- writer — documentation, reports, paper text.
